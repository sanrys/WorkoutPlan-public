Workout Plan V2

V2 hybrid split for leg growth, upper-body progress, cardio, and recovery.

V2 Hybrid

Missed-Day Reset

BridgeWhen days are skipped or shifted, use the next sensible session based on fatigue.
Protect legsKeep Tuesday as quad day and Thursday as posterior-chain day. Do not stack hard leg days.
WednesdayUse Wednesday as the reset anchor: Zone 2, core, mobility, then resume the normal schedule.

Progressive Overload

CompoundsAdd 2.5-5 lbs only after all reps are clean with 1-2 reps in reserve.
IsolationAdd reps first, then small weight increases once the top of the range is owned.
LegsPrioritize controlled reps, knee comfort, and recovery over chasing load every week.

V2 block starts 2026-07-05. Week 5 is a deload check: reduce sets 30-40% only if performance, joints, sleep, or motivation are trending down.

Conditioning

After liftUse 10-20 min easy Zone 2 after upper days when recovery allows.
LongerUse 25-45 min Zone 2 on Wednesday or Saturday for visceral-fat support.
IntervalsGrit Runner intervals are optional, 0-1x/week, only when legs feel fresh.
AbsBefore lifting, keep core to activation. Do harder ab work on Wednesday or Friday.

Knee Notes

Squat depthStop around parallel. No need to chase depth if the right lateral knee gets irritated.
Single legKeep step-ups and single-leg press smooth, controlled, and pain-free through the full set.
AdjustIf a movement talks back, shorten range of motion or use a light leg extension in a clean range.
