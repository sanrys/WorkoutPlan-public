Workout Plan

6-day push/pull plan for strength, muscle, legs, and fat loss.

Equinox

Progressive Overload

A daysStrength focus. Add 2.5-5 lbs after all reps are clean with 1-2 reps in reserve.
B daysHypertrophy focus. Own the top of the 8-15 rep target before adding weight.
C daysPower focus. Use mostly 5-8 reps; add weight every 2-3 weeks when speed stays high.

Deload every 4-6 weeks by reducing volume about 40%. Deload earlier if performance drops for 2+ sessions.

Conditioning

Grit RunnerUse it like a sled substitute: 6-8 rounds of 20 sec hard effort, then 70-100 sec easy walk.
Zone 2For visceral fat support, aim for 2-4 steady sessions weekly at 25-45 min.
TimingKeep hard intervals away from the day before heavy squats or deadlifts when legs feel beat up.

Knee Notes

Squat depthStop around parallel. No need to chase depth if the right lateral knee gets irritated.
Single legKeep step-ups and single-leg press smooth, controlled, and pain-free through the full set.
AdjustIf a movement talks back, shorten range of motion or use a light leg extension in a clean range.
